Common questions

I bought Pro but it isn't showing.

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel the monthly plan?

On your iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Bajada. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

Someone called the score wrong.

Tap Undo on the scoreboard. You can undo as many points as you need, even after the match ends, before you save it.

What is Star Point?

At deuce you play up to two advantages. If it comes back to deuce a third time, the next point wins the game.

Can I move my matches to a new phone?

Matches are stored on the device and included in your iPhone backup. Restoring the backup on a new phone brings them with it.
